PRODUCT AND REALITY BOUNDARY
FreightIQ is a maritime freight-rate forecasting and vessel-chartering decision-support prototype for Indian steel manufacturers, procurement leads, chartering teams, shipping analysts and logistics decision-makers. Users review a shipment, inspect synthetic route/benchmark market signals, forecast freight, rank modeled vessel/date candidates, test disruptions, inspect simulated downside, backtest a decision rule, and audit underlying data and assumptions. It is currently a Python Streamlit multipage application with 11 reachable pages: Home plus 10 numbered pages. Plotly charts, native forms/tabs/dataframes, PDF/CSV downloads, local SQLite decision and audit records, session-held shipment/scenario context, and a light/dark theme already exist. The design should look like a bespoke world-class product while staying implementable from those actual capabilities. Most freight histories, market signals, forecasts and evaluation results are SYNTHETIC/DEMO; cached public Open-Meteo wind observations are a separate external weather-method validation. Do not imply live freight feeds, vessel positions, AIS tracking, booking, automated procurement, real-world forecast accuracy, or fully synchronized cross-page model baselines. The existing charter route card is a text/arrow diagram, not a geographic map.

DESIGN VISION
Create a futuristic Maritime Intelligence Command Center: the information density and rigor of a financial terminal, operational clarity of a command center, interaction polish of a modern productivity tool, and visual cues from marine charts and ocean navigation. Make it recognizably FreightIQ and do not copy another product. The experience should feel precise, calm, expensive and decision-ready. Use luminous accents only to guide attention; avoid decorative glow, neon overload, stock ship photography, skeuomorphic gauges, generic admin cards and gratuitous glass blur. Show evidence next to conclusions. Make provenance, unit and time scope as visible as the number itself.

FOUNDATION / DESIGN TOKENS

Electric cyan #35D6E8 for the primary analytical focus; ocean turquoise #36D6B2 for route and selection accents. Emerald for favorable or confirmed-in-scope states, amber for caution/coverage gaps, coral red for critical or adverse states. Use color with icon/text/shape, never color alone. Reserve bright cyan for active chart series, selected tabs and primary actions; maintain a restrained hierarchy. Provide a fully coherent light counterpart if theme switching is represented; all charts, tables, controls, overlays and exports need matching themes.
Typography: Geist, Inter or Manrope; crisp sentence-case labels; monospaced or tabular numerals for ₹, USD, dates, rates, coordinates/IDs and quantities. Strong page titles; compact uppercase eyebrow labels only where useful; readable 14–16 px body and dense table text that remains legible. Use an 8 px spacing grid, 12–16 px card radii, fine dividers, generous chart plotting area and purposeful whitespace. Prefer precise lines, subtle depth and restrained hover/selection motion (about 150–220 ms). No vague KPI labels; always expose denominator, units and period.
