PRODUCT
FreightIQ forecasts freight rates, ranks modeled vessel/date charter choices, stress-tests hypothetical disruptions and evaluates decision rules. It is a decision-support prototype using predominantly synthetic demo data, not a live vessel-tracking or charter-execution platform. Keep INR, crore/lakh, USD where used, tonnes, hours/days, dates, data period and provenance explicit. Robustness /100 is a composite score, not a probability.
